Our Process

"Understanding clients' existing mutual fund portfolios and drawing out a distribution of funds, basis the inherent fund asset class, and simultaneously matching it with client risk profile."

Phase I

Portfolio Forensics

We conduct a granular examination of existing equity, debt, and fund holdings to uncover hidden fees, asset overlaps, and unintended risk exposure.

Phase II

Risk & Allocation Matching

Structuring a balanced distribution of funds calibrated precisely to your time horizon, drawdown tolerance, and specific family cash-flow milestones.

Fiduciary Oversight

Continuous active monitoring, disciplined rebalancing, and transparent periodic reviews ensuring your holdings remain aligned as markets evolve.
