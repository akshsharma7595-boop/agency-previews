Institutional Risk Protocol

The 7-Point SMC Gatekeeper

Why funded accounts blow up is never strategy—it is taking B-tier and C-tier discretionary setups. LiquiQuant OS enforces an uncompromising institutional checklist:

1

Higher Timeframe (HTF) Narrative & Daily Bias

Is price drawing on sell-side or buy-side liquidity on the 4H/Daily chart? Trade ONLY in the direction of the macro draw on liquidity.

2

Session Liquidity Purge (Sweep of Asian / Prev Day High/Low)

No entry in the middle of a consolidation. Has clean buy-side or sell-side retail liquidity been aggressively swept?

3

SMT Divergence Confirmation (Correlated Asset Decoupling)

EURUSD vs GBPUSD or NAS100 vs ES: Did one make a new swing high/low while the other failed? Institutional divergence must be confirmed.

4

Market Structure Shift (MSS) with Energetic Displacement

Body close through the swing point leaving a clean Fair Value Gap (FVG). Slow wicks do NOT qualify as an institutional displacement.

5

Optimal Trade Entry (OTE / FVG Tap) within Killzone Timing

Execution restricted strictly to London Killzone (2:00 AM - 5:00 AM EST) or New York AM Killzone (8:00 AM - 11:00 AM EST). No trades outside algorithmic windows.

6

Mathematical Risk Validation (≤ 0.5% Account Equity)

Lot size must be calculated strictly off stop-loss pips using the Drawdown Shield. Sizing up after a loss is programmatically locked.

7

Minimum 1:2.5 Asymmetrical Risk-to-Reward Ratio

Targeting opposing resting liquidity pools. If target is less than 2.5R, the order ticket is cancelled.
